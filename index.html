How to play
  • Goal: get every mouse into a rocket.
  • Mice step every ¼ s; cats move at half speed.
  • Blocked? Creatures turn clockwise. Arrows redirect mice and cats.
  • Lose if a cat catches any mouse or reaches a rocket.
  • Place arrows before pressing Start. Reset keeps your arrows.
